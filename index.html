A card duel you can stand inside

ASTRAVEIL is a VR trading card game. Build a deck, strap on your Duel Disk, and watch every monster you summon rise up life-size in front of you.

100 summonable monsters

Every monster card has its own 3D model, entrance and attacks - dragons, krakens, golems, sirens and more.

Online duels with voice

Challenge friends with a lobby code or jump into a random battle, with voice chat and emotes across the table.

Ranked seasons

Climb from Bronze to Diamond, unlock ranked arenas, Duel Disks and card backs, and top the leaderboard.

Duelist Ladder

Fight a ladder of AI rivals offline, all the way up to the Abyss King, with a full tutorial to learn the rules.

Your style

Duel Disks, card backs, titles and arena auras - from clean Common gear to Ultra Rare showpieces with rune rings and shimmer.

Play together on Steam and Epic

Steam and Epic Games Store players duel each other in the same matches.
