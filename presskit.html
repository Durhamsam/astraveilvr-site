Description

ASTRAVEIL is a VR trading card game where every monster you summon rises life-size in front of you. Draw a card from the Duel Disk on your wrist, throw it onto the field and watch your monster rise in a pillar of light, then walk out among your monsters and command them in battle in front of a roaring stadium crowd.

Duels are fast and easy to read, and a hands-on tutorial teaches every rule in a few minutes. Every win ends with a finishing move: each of the game's 23 kinds of monster finishes its rival in its own way, from a crab that rips its opponent in half to a dragon that burns it to ash.

Features

  • 100 monsters you can stand next to. Every monster card has its own 3D model, and they never stand still: crabs snap their claws, dragons beat their wings, wolves pant and wag.
  • A finishing move for every kind. 23 kinds of monster, 23 finishers. No blood: beaten monsters burst into light.
  • Summons with spectacle. Each kind arrives in its own way, and Ultra Rares get a full Legendary Summon.
  • Walk the arena. Move across the field among your monsters with the joysticks.
  • 150 cards. Monsters, spells and traps across five rarities, with holographic Ultra Rares. Card packs are bought with gold earned by playing.
  • Ways to duel. A ladder of AI rivals with boss duels, online duels by lobby code, ranked matchmaking from Bronze to Diamond, and 10-minute Timed Duels.
  • Crossplay. Steam and Epic Games Store players duel each other, with voice chat and emotes.
  • Cosmetics, never power. Card sleeves, titles, field auras and Duel Disk styles. Nothing for sale makes a deck stronger.
